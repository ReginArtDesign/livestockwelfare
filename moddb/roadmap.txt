COMING IN THE NEXT UPDATE

Baby animals
- Lambs, kids, piglets and chicks are now part of the mod.
- Lambs, kids and piglets suckle from their mother, so her condition matters: a thirsty, sick or stressed mother has less milk, and a sick mother can pass her illness on to her baby.
- Poorly fed babies grow more slowly; starving ones stop healing and can die of malnutrition.
- Babies feel the cold and the heat sooner than adults.
- A baby keeps its age and health when it grows up.

Orphans and hand feeding
- If the mother dies, her baby becomes an orphan. A newborn does not know how to eat from a trough yet.
- Feed it by hand: sneak and hold right click with milk (bucket or jug) or with food it eats (grain, vegetables...). The baby takes the food from your hand and chews it.
- After three feedings by hand (or a couple of days spent with its mother) it learns to eat from a trough.

Cleaner, calmer care
- Babies never get dirty: their mother keeps them clean. Chicks don't get dirty either, only their pen does.
- Chickens clean themselves in clean surroundings and don't need grooming.
- Animals lie down while you groom them and get up once they are clean, so they don't wander off mid-brush.

Sickness
- Flies now only mean dirt. A sick animal shows it by being listless and lying down often, and a very sick one stays down until it recovers.

Hot sun
- Animals in the hot sun lose their appetite: they skip the trough until it is cooler or they are in the shade.
- After a while in the heat they become listless and lie down now and then, so give them shade.

A safe place to sleep
- Kept animals with no roof over them at night have no safe place to sleep, and that stresses them. Wild animals don't mind.
- "Weather stress" is now simply "stress", as not all of it comes from the weather. The info text still names each cause: getting wet, cold, hot sun, no safe place to sleep.

Compatibility
- Hydrate or Diedrate support: animals drink its clean rainwater, well water, distilled and boiled water (from barrels too), but not its salt, muddy, tainted or poisoned water.

Fixes
- Wild animals no longer die of thirst, bad weather or disease: only kept animals (placed by you or born in your care) can. Wild ones still get thirsty, stressed and sick, they just don't die of it.
- Animals, chickens above all, can now drink from ponds whose water lies a block below the bank. Before, they stood at the edge and never got close enough.
- Muck no longer floats in the air when the ground under it is dug out: it breaks and drops its rot.
- Muck no longer leaves a see-through hole in the block under it (which could be used as x-ray).
- Animals no longer give up walking to a water barrel when the way there is tricky, which could leave them dying of thirst next to water.
- Animals drink after time skips (sleeping, /time add, unloaded areas).
- Sneak + right click (petting, checking an animal) no longer makes the game stutter.

PLANNED FOR LATER

- Milking vs. nursing: milking a nursing mother leaves less milk for her baby.
- Support for other mods' animals: Teapot's Giant Boar, Great Bighorn and Giant Redriver are done; next come Traditional Livestock, Feverstone Wilds, The Critters Pack, Equus (horses), Genealogy, FotSA Bovinae and Cuniculture. Smaller critters will get a lighter set of needs, since some mechanics would just be annoying at that size.
- Rotting carcasses: after a while flies gather over a dead animal (wild or kept), a sign that its meat is no longer fresh, and butchering it then gives rot instead of meat.
- In-game settings menu (with the ConfigLib mod), so you can tune the animals' needs without editing files.
- Testing in existing world saves and in multiplayer.
