Livestock Welfare

Your animals need more than a fence.

Sheep, goats, pigs and chickens grow old, get dirty and sick, look for shelter from rain and sun, and walk to the water barrel when they're thirsty. Keep them clean, groomed and cared for, and they thrive.

Love spending your days with your animals more than anything else? This mod is for you. It adds so much depth to keeping and breeding livestock that you'll grow attached to them - be warned, culling that old ram won't feel as easy anymore.

WHAT YOUR ANIMALS NEED

Five things a good farmer watches

Age

  • Every animal has a lifespan: chickens 2–3 years, pigs 3–4, sheep 3–5, goats 4–6.
  • Old animals stop breeding in the last quarter of their life.
  • Let one die of old age and its carcass gives only 40%. Cull in time.

Cleanliness & health

  • Crowded pens and muck make animals filthy: mud spots on the coat, flies around them.
  • Filthy animals fall sick, can infect others of their kind, and stop healing.
  • Very sick animals lie down; clean surroundings help them recover.

Shelter

  • Rain, snow, cold and strong sun stress animals in the open; stress brings sickness.
  • In bad weather they walk under the nearest roof, and at night they sleep there.
  • A closed barn also keeps out the cold. Wool helps: sheep handle −10 °C, pigs only +2 °C.

Water

  • Thirst grows over time, faster when it's warm and in the hot sun.
  • Thirsty animals walk to a pond, river or water barrel and drink there, head down.
  • Ponds are free; barrels really empty (about 8 litres a day for 10 sheep). Refill them.

Care

  • Groom with dry grass: sneak and hold right click. The dirt falls off.
  • Pet with an empty hand to calm a stressed animal.
  • Shovel the muck out of the pen. You get rot for compost, and the shovel never hurts them.
  

HOW IT PLAYS

A day in the pen

 06:00The flock wakes up under the roof and walks out to graze. One ewe is thirsty and heads straight for the water barrel.
 11:00Rain sets in. The sheep in the open walk back under the roof and wait it out. Those caught outside start to get stressed.
 15:00The pen is getting crowded and muck builds up near the trough. You shovel it out and groom the dirtiest ewe with a bundle of dry grass.
 19:00An old ram has stopped breeding. You cull him while he still gives full meat and hide.
 22:00Night. Every animal finds a dry spot deep under the roof and lies down to sleep.

FOR SERVERS

Gentler when you need it

Many players asked for a way to play without losing animals. In ModConfig/livestockwelfare.json:

  • AnimalsCanDie: turn death off completely. Animals still get old, sick and weak, but stay at 1 health.
  • OldAgeCanKill, DiseaseCanKill, ExposureCanKill, ThirstCanKill: per cause.
  • PauseWhenNoPlayerNearby: no aging or needs while nobody is within 64 blocks, so animals don't suffer while their owner is offline.

Every system (aging, hygiene, weather, thirst) has its own switch and settings.

GOOD TO KNOW

Compatibility

  • Covers vanilla sheep, goats, pigs and chickens (adults).
  • Petting also triggers Better Husbandry's petting.
  • Animals from genelib mods (e.g. Detailed Animals) may not show mud spots; everything else works.
  • Other mods' animals, horses included, are not covered yet.
  • Not tested yet in existing world saves (added to a world that was already running) or in multiplayer. Back up your world first, and please report anything odd.

This is a beta. Balance values are first guesses: feedback from long-running worlds is very welcome.

OPEN ABOUT IT

How this mod was made

Livestock Welfare is designed and tested by ReginArtDesign and written with the help of an AI assistant, Claude by Anthropic. I'm not a programmer - I'm a junior web and UX designer. I came up with the ideas, decided how the animals should behave, designed this page and tested every feature in game. The illustrations on this page were made with AI.

The AI wrote the code, looked up how the base game works (reading the game's own code to follow its rules for rooms, rain and animal AI) and ran automated tests on a test server. Ideas from the Vintage Story community shaped many features, like the safety options for servers.
