Version 0.16.0 (beta)

Note: Livestock Welfare is still in beta. Please back up your world before updating, and tell me if something goes wrong. New settings are added to your config file automatically; the values you changed are kept.

Fauna of the Stone Age support (requested by a player)
- If you play with the Fauna of the Stone Age mods, their animals now have welfare needs too: thirst, a roof for the night and bad weather, cleanliness and disease, old age, and nursing for the young. Without those mods nothing changes.
- Bovinae, Elephantidae and Cervinae: aurochs and cattle, bison, buffaloes, antelopes, elephants, mammoths and the giant deer, wild, semi-tamed and tamed, with their calves.
- A calf you raised by hand counts as your own animal once it grows up semi-tamed, even if it was born in the wild.
- Caninae, Felinae, Pantherinae, Machairodontinae, Viverridae and Manidae, which you can keep with Tamables: FOTSA: wolves, jackals, lynxes, caracals, lions, tigers, cave lions, snow leopards, sabre-tooths and more.
- Dinornithidae: moas and adzebills, plus kiwis, ducks, geese, rails and kakapos. Their chicks hatch from eggs, so like chicken chicks they neither suckle nor drink.
- Smaller animals (foxes, civets, pangolins, kiwis, ducks, rails, kakapos...) get a lighter set without dirt and disease, since grooming them would just be annoying.
- Each group has its own lifespan, the temperatures it copes with (a woolly mammoth or an arctic fox shrugs off the cold, a pangolin or a desert fox does not) and how much it drinks. You can change them in the config.
- Sea turtles, the river dolphin and the tiniest birds are left out.

Hay bale (requested by a player)
- Craft a hay bale from four hay blocks and set it down in the pen. Sheep, goats and other grass eaters walk to it and eat from it like from a trough.
- It holds four portions and gets smaller with each one. Broken, it gives back the hay that is left.
