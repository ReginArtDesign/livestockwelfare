TITLE:
Livestock Welfare – Sheep go to bed under the roof | Vintage Story mod

DESCRIPTION:
When evening comes, the sheep stop grazing and walk under a roof to sleep there until morning. A roof over their heads also keeps off rain, snow and the hot sun, so give your animals a sheltered place to rest.

Coming in the next update: kept animals with no roof over them at night have no safe place to sleep, and that stresses them.

Livestock Welfare makes your farm animals need real care: clean pens, fresh water, shelter from the weather and a safe place to sleep. Neglected animals get stressed, sick and can die.

TITLE:
Livestock Welfare – Muck builds up in the pen | Vintage Story mod

DESCRIPTION:
Besides their droppings, kept animals slowly trample muck into the ground where they stand. It builds up in layers, and faster when too many animals are crowded together. Muck and droppings make the animals dirty, and dirty animals fall sick more easily.

Shovel it up to keep the pen clean: it drops rot. Shovels don't hurt the animals, they just nudge them aside.

=== VIDEO 4 ===

TITLE:
Livestock Welfare – A clay waterer for the chickens | Vintage Story mod

DESCRIPTION:
Chickens get thirsty too, and they need water they can reach. The clay waterer is made for them: shape it on the clay forming grid, fire it in a pit kiln and fill it with a bucket. It holds 10 litres, enough for about 10 hens for over a week. Thirsty hens walk to it, dip their beak into the bowl and lift it to swallow, with little drops in the water. If there is a pond or a puddle nearby, they would rather drink from that.

What else your chickens need in Livestock Welfare:
- Dust baths: chickens don't need grooming. They walk to dry sand and take a dust bath in a cloud of sand to get clean. Sand under the open sky turns to mud while it rains, but under a roof it always works. Without dry sand nearby they get scruffy.
- Shelter: rain, snow, cold below 0 °C and the hot sun above 30 °C stress them, so in bad weather they walk under the nearest roof. At night they go under the roof to sleep, as kept animals with no roof over them have no safe place to rest.
- A clean coop: hens leave muck on the ground, faster when they are crowded. Dirty surroundings make them sick, and sick hens lose health, don't breed and can pass it on to the others. The info text tells you why a hen is sick: the dirt, the bad weather, or another animal.
- Old age: hens live 2 to 3 in-game years. Old hens no longer breed, and in the end they die of old age.
- Chicks: they don't get dirty, only their pen does, but they feel the cold and the heat sooner than adults.

Livestock Welfare makes your farm animals need real care: clean pens, fresh water, shelter from the weather and a safe place to sleep. Neglected animals get stressed, sick and can die.

=== VIDEO 5 ===

TITLE:
Livestock Welfare – Hens follow the grain, and a coop kit | Vintage Story mod

DESCRIPTION:
Hold some grain and your hens come running after you, instead of running away like they usually do. They still flee from foxes and wolves, and when they get hurt. Sneak and hold right click on a hen to feed her from your hand: she takes the grain and eats it. It counts as a meal from the trough, so it helps them breed too, and then she is full for an hour.

The chicken coop kit is crafted from planks and two hen boxes. Right-click the ground and a small raised coop is built right there, stretching away from you: four posts, a pitched roof, a little ramp up to the pop hole and two hen boxes inside. Steps lead up to a gate at the back, so you can walk in to collect the eggs. It has room for 6 to 8 hens, and there is shelter underneath too. It needs a clear spot of 5 by 9 blocks, 6 high.

Livestock Welfare makes your farm animals need real care: clean pens, fresh water, shelter from the weather and a safe place to sleep. Neglected animals get stressed, sick and can die.
