=== VIDEO 1 ===

TITLE:
Livestock Welfare – Sheep go to bed under the roof | Vintage Story mod

DESCRIPTION:
When evening comes, the sheep stop grazing and walk under a roof to sleep there until morning. A roof over their heads also keeps off rain, snow and the hot sun, so give your animals a sheltered place to rest.

Coming in the next update: kept animals with no roof over them at night have no safe place to sleep, and that stresses them.

Livestock Welfare makes your farm animals need real care: clean pens, fresh water, shelter from the weather and a safe place to sleep. Neglected animals get stressed, sick and can die.

=== VIDEO 2 ===

TITLE:
Livestock Welfare – Grooming dirty sheep | Vintage Story mod

DESCRIPTION:
Animals kept in a dirty pen get dirty themselves: you can see it on their coat, and flies start buzzing around them. Dirty animals fall sick more easily.

To clean them, hold a handful of dry grass, sneak and hold right click on the animal to rub the dirt off.

Coming in the next update: animals lie down while you groom them and get up once they are clean, so they don't wander off mid-brush.

=== VIDEO 3 ===

TITLE:
Livestock Welfare – Muck builds up in the pen | Vintage Story mod

DESCRIPTION:
Besides their droppings, kept animals slowly trample muck into the ground where they stand. It builds up in layers, and faster when too many animals are crowded together. Muck and droppings make the animals dirty, and dirty animals fall sick more easily.

Shovel it up to keep the pen clean: it drops rot. Shovels don't hurt the animals, they just nudge them aside.

Livestock Welfare makes your farm animals need real care: clean pens, fresh water, shelter from the weather and a safe place to sleep. Neglected animals get stressed, sick and can die.
